INT. PROFESSIONS TO EXPLORE IN TECH - FUTURE

A TECHNICAL WRITER (30s), a UI/UX DESIGNER (30s), and a JUNIOR DEVELOPER (30s) walk into a bar.

JUNIOR DEVELOPER

Who puts a pull handle on a push door?

UI/UX DESIGNER

Classic poor design. You just did what the handle told you to do.

TECHNICAL WRITER

All it needs is a sign that says “push.” Problem solved in one word.

The BARTENDER just looks at them, annoyed.

The door RATTLES — another MAN struggles to figure it out and open it. The three turn to the Bartender — see?

UI/UX DESIGNER

You really should redesign the handle.

TECHNICAL WRITER

Put up a sign.

JUNIOR DEVELOPER

Open a ticket with the building.

BARTENDER

Not my bar, not my problem.
